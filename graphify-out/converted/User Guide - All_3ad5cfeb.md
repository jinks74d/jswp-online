## Topic Sentence Development
- For each of your CDs, ask the question, “What is positive about this CD?” Brainstorm as many positive attributes as possible for each CD and list them on the left side of the chart.
- Once you’ve exhausted that list, for each of your CDs, ask a new question, “What is negative about this CD?” Brainstorm as many negative attributes as possible for each CD and list them on the right side of the chart.
- Choose a side.
- On the side you choose, select the TS you are going to use.
- On the other side, choose the counterargument that works.

## Working Topic Sentence

- Make a sentence out of your blue TS thought from the previous screen.
- Remember, it’s still a first draft.


## Generating Commentary

- Think about each of your CDs on the left. Ask yourself, “What is important about this particular CD that helps me to support my TS?”

- Brainstorm your thoughts and enter the CM boxes.
- For however many CDs you selected on the first page, you’ll be ask to generate commentary ideas.

- Insight: Sometimes one CD will generate a great amount of commentary; other times, it won’t. That’s why you might want to combine one or two on the first page.

## First Draft

- Using the green CMs that you brainstormed, you will
- Revise your topic sentence;
- Update your concession and counterargument;
- Write your refutation;
- Write your commentary sentence; and
- Write your concluding sentence.
- Remember, “when you use it, you lose it,” so click on the CMs you use, so you’ll not use them more than once.
